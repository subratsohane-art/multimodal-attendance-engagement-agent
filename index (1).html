The Problem

Manual Roll Calls & Blind Engagement Spots

In educational institutions and professional training environments, traditional attendance tracking is prone to proxy fraud and proxy presence. Moreover, basic biometric systems fail to capture meaningful engagement context, leaving instructors blind to student attention drops, interaction lulls, or pacing issues.

The AI Agent Solution

Sustained Presence & Multimodal Fusion

Our system continuously fuses live video head pose, motion tracking, audio energy dynamics, and sustained presence logic to automatically output accurate timestamped attendance logs and live teaching recommendations—without transmitting personal camera feeds outside the local device.

Operational Scenarios

Designed for Complex Classroom Environments

Three foundational workflows designed to assist educators without intruding on pedagogical freedom.

01

Automated Attendance Tracking

During a session, the agent processes video to detect and track participants. Attendance is marked strictly on sustained presence rather than single-frame detections, completely eliminating false positives caused by temporary visitors walking by the room.

  • Timestamped entry/exit logs
  • Bounding-box temporal persistence
  • Zero manual roll-call overhead
02

Engagement & Session Dynamics

Fuses behavioral indicators including head pose yaw/pitch, body stillness, inactivity duration, and ambient room acoustics. Integrates these signals across custom time windows to spot attention drops, peak interaction, and overall pacing issues.

  • Head pose & gaze angle estimation
  • Inactivity & idle duration detection
  • Classroom acoustic energy fusion
03

AI-Driven Teaching Suggestions

Based on classroom dynamics, the agent provides actionable, context-aware suggestions in real time. The system acts strictly as an assistive decision-support tool, empowering instructors to pivot pacing or deploy interactive quizzes.

  • Real-time teaching strategy alerts
  • Non-intrusive HUD dashboard feed
  • Human-in-the-loop governance
